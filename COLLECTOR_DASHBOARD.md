## 2. Taluk & Village Drill-Down

- **Taluk Officers Scorecard:** Evaluates Tahsildars, Block Development Officers (BDO), and Taluk Supply Officers.
- **Ration & Essential Commodity Stocks:** Live status of fair price shops (FPS / Ration shops) and stock availability.
- **Emergency Disaster Activation:** One-touch alert mobilization for Revenue, Police, Fire & Rescue, and Medical teams.
