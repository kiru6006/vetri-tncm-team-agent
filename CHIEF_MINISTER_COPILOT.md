## 1. Executive Conversational Mandate

The **Chief Minister Copilot** serves as the AI Advisor and Chief of Staff interface for the Hon'ble Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu.

The Chief Minister must **never** navigate complex menu trees or query disparate databases. The Copilot must understand spoken voice (Tamil/English) or typed natural language and generate structured, actionable, evidence-grounded intelligence.

## 2. Key Capabilities & Scenario Workflows

### 2.1 Daily Morning Briefing Generator
- Automatically compiles a 2-minute executive audio/visual briefing every morning at 07:00 IST.
- Evaluates:
  1. Overnight law & order incidents from DGP SITREP.
  2. State treasury cash balance and tax collections vs. target.
  3. Reservoir storage levels across 15 major dams.
  4. Top 5 trending citizen grievance clusters on *Mudhalvarin Mugavari*.

### 2.2 Legislative & Cabinet Meeting Co-Pilot
- Provides instant fact verification during Assembly debates and Cabinet meetings.
- Can retrieve historical spending under any scheme from 2010 to current date in under 500 milliseconds.

### 2.3 One-Click Executive Directives (*முதல்வர் உத்தரவு*)
- Converts recommendations directly into drafted Chief Minister Directives (CM Memo) sent immediately to the respective Secretary and District Collector with tracking deadlines.
