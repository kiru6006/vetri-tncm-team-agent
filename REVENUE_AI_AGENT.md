## 1. Domain Agent Mandate

The **Revenue AI Agent** monitors all state revenue streams in real time, detecting anomalies, forecasting quarterly collections, and uncovering tax leakage patterns across business sectors and geographic zones.

### Core Capabilities
- **GST & E-Way Bill Anomaly Detection:** Flags circular trading networks and fake invoicing rings across industrial clusters (e.g., textile hubs in Tirupur, engineering in Coimbatore, automotive in Sriperumbudur).
- **Property Registration Discrepancy Finder:** Compares guideline values vs. reported transaction amounts using spatial valuation models.
- **Revenue Leakage Forecasting:** Predictive machine learning models alerting the Finance Secretary 30 days before revenue shortfalls occur.
