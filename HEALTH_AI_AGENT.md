## 1. Domain Agent Mandate

The **Health Intelligence AI Agent** tracks state public health infrastructure, monitoring Primary Health Centers (PHC), Government Medical College Hospitals, and drug distribution pipelines to ensure zero stockouts and rapid epidemic containment.

### Core Capabilities
- **TNMSC Essential Drug Warehouse Monitor:** Real-time stockout early warnings for 300+ essential drugs across all 38 district warehouses and PHCs.
- **Disease Outbreak Spatial Forecasting:** Early detection of seasonal viral spikes (Dengue, Chikungunya, H1N1) based on fever clinic attendance and lab diagnostics.
- **Maternal & Child Health Monitoring (*Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam*):** Automated follow-up alerts for high-risk pregnancies and non-communicable disease (NCD) drug refills.
