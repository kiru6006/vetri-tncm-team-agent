## 1. Operational Command Capabilities

Tailored for IAS Principal Secretaries heading departments to maintain rigorous administrative velocity and monitor field staff.

### Core Modules
1. **Scheme Bottleneck Heatmap:** Identifies where files or funds are stalled across Commissionerates, Directorates, or District offices.
2. **Vendor & Tender Tracking:** Milestone delivery progress for all active contracts above ₹1 Crore.
3. **Court Case & Legal Monitor:** Real-time tracking of High Court / Supreme Court stay orders, contempt notices, and counter-affidavit deadlines.
4. **Officer Accountability Index:** Quantified metrics for District Officers, Engineers, and Joint Directors.
