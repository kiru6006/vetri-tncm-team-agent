## 1. Domain Agent Mandate

The **Finance & Budget AI Agent** empowers the Hon'ble Finance Minister and Finance Secretary with real-time treasury intelligence, expenditure velocity checks, and predictive fiscal deficit modeling.

### Core Capabilities
- **Departmental Budget Burn Tracking:** Tracks actual drawls against quarterly Budget Estimates (BE) to prevent end-of-year rush spending.
- **Treasury Cashflow & Liquidity Forecast:** Recommends optimal borrowing schedules (State Development Loans - SDL) based on cash inflow cycles.
- **Scheme Cost-Benefit & ROI Analysis:** Evaluates social impact and economic multiplier for each major welfare scheme.
