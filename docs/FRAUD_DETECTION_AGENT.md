## 1. Domain Agent Mandate

The **Fraud Detection & Anomaly AI Agent** acts as an autonomous integrity watchdog, scanning transactions across welfare distribution schemes, public procurement tenders, and civil works billing.

### Core Capabilities
- **Ghost Beneficiary Identification:** Cross-checks DBT recipient rosters against death registries, active tax-payer rolls, and duplicate bank accounts.
- **Tender Bid Rigging & Collusion Detection:** Analyzes contractor bidding patterns, IP address footprints, and joint-venture corporate linkages to detect cartel behavior.
- **Inflated Works & Measurement Book (MB) Verification:** Flags civil works where billing exceeds physical satellite progress or historical material price curves.
