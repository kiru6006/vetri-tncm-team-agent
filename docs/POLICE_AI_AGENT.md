## 1. Domain Agent Mandate

The **Police & Public Safety AI Agent** assists the DGP and Home Secretary in analyzing state-wide security trends, pending investigation aging, accident hotspots, and emergency mobilization.

### Core Capabilities
- **Crime Trend & Cluster Detection:** Spatial clustering of property crimes, cybercrime reports, and narcotics seizures.
- **Accident Hotspot Mitigation:** Integration with Highway Patrol telemetry to recommend speed calming, lighting, or patrol shifts on national/state highways.
- **SITREP (Daily Situation Report) Synthesizer:** Aggregates 1,500+ police station daily logs into a 1-page executive summary for the Chief Minister and Home Secretary.
