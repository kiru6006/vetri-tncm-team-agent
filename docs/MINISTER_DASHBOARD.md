## 1. Portfolio Command Center Overview

Designed for Cabinet Ministers overseeing designated state ministries (e.g., Finance, Health, Industries, Agriculture, School Education, Transport).

### Key Functional Capabilities
- **Portfolio Health Card:** Aggregate score for all departments operating under the Minister's purview.
- **Scheme Execution & Beneficiary Outreach:** Live coverage tracking of welfare schemes vs. annual targets.
- **Legislative Question Co-Pilot:** Instant generation of accurate, verified statistical data for Assembly queries (*சட்டமன்ற வினாக்கள்*).
- **Budget Utilization Telemetry:** Real-time tracking of capital outlay (CAPEX) vs. operational expenses (OPEX).
